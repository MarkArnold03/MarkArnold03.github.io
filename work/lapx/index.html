  1. 06/2026
    Delivered

    In daily production use by around 100 people in transport, moving, warehouse and assembly.

  2. Releases

    Owned the release chain: build, versioning, verified IIS deploys, rollback and production troubleshooting. Grew the xUnit suite to 450+ tests.

  3. Field app

    A PWA for drivers, warehouse staff and installers: clock in and out with GPS geofence, offline queue in IndexedDB, QR scanning, damage reports with photos, 9 languages.

  4. Frontend

    JavaScript SPA for staffing, dispatch, live map, warehouse and time reporting, plus an in-app AI assistant on the Claude API.

  5. Real-time

    SignalR and Server-Sent Events for staffing, attendance, shifts and permission changes.

  6. Time & HR

    Working hours with automatic breaks, overtime approval, comp-time balance, absence and Excel export.

  7. Integrations

    Background services for Opter TMS, Ongoing WMS (API and webhooks), Movex, Business Central and Keatech vehicle GPS. Leads fetched and parsed from email via Microsoft Graph and IMAP.

  8. Access

    A four-level role model with JWT and refresh tokens, permissions per module and audit logging.

  9. Backend

    Business-critical backend in .NET 9, ASP.NET Core Web API, Clean Architecture, EF Core and SQL Server, with about 90 API controllers.
